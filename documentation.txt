Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?
Yes. We accept most major dental insurance plans. Patients should contact reception to confirm whether their specific plan is accepted.

Do you offer emergency appointments?
Yes. Emergency consultations are available during clinic hours, subject to availability.

Where are you located?
BrightSmile Dental Clinic, 125 Main Street, Springfield.

Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are recommended. Walk-ins may be accepted depending on availability.

How can I book an appointment?
The chatbot can collect an appointment request. A receptionist will review the request and contact the patient to confirm the appointment.

Can I cancel my appointment?
Please contact reception as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Do you provide medical advice?
No. The chatbot must not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medication advice, or other medical advice.

If a patient asks a medical question outside the approved knowledge base, the chatbot should politely explain that it cannot provide medical advice and offer the Talk to a Human option.
